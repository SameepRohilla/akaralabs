Two ways we print

Filament for function, resin for fine detail.

Process · FDM

Fused deposition

Molten filament, laid layer by layer

The workhorse. Engineering-grade thermoplastics built up in layers down to 0.1mm — strong, functional parts in a wide range of polymers. Best for prototypes, enclosures, brackets and production runs.

PLA · PETG · ASA · ABS · PA-CF · TPU 0.1–0.3mm layers
Process · SLA

Stereolithography

Liquid resin cured by light

For when detail matters more than load. Photopolymer resin cured at 0.05mm gives glass-smooth surfaces and crisp fine geometry — masters, miniatures, jewellery patterns and presentation models.

The materials

Seven we reach for most.

Ratings are relative — a guide, not a datasheet.

PLA Polylactic acid FDM

Crisp, easy, low-cost — the natural first draft.

Prints cleanly with sharp detail and bright colour. Rigid but brittle, and it softens in heat or sun — so it's ideal indoors, on the bench, and for anything you just need to see and hold.

PETG Glycol-modified PET FDM

The everyday workhorse — tougher, and forgiving.

Stronger and less brittle than PLA, with some flex and good resistance to moisture and mild chemicals. Our default when a part has to actually do a job rather than just be looked at.

ASA Acrylonitrile styrene acrylate FDM

Built for the outdoors and the sun.

UV-stable and weather-resistant, holding colour and strength where PLA and PETG would yellow or warp. Tolerates real heat. Our go-to for anything that lives outside or under a hot enclosure.

ABS Acrylonitrile butadiene styrene FDM

Tough, heat-tolerant, and smoothable.

The classic engineering plastic — impact-resistant and good with heat, and it can be vapour-smoothed to a glossy, injection-moulded look. It needs a controlled print environment as it's prone to warping, so we run it in an enclosure.

PA-CF Carbon-fibre-filled nylon FDM

Stiff, light and heat-tough — the engineering pick.

Carbon fibre in a nylon matrix gives an exceptional stiffness-to-weight ratio and high heat resistance. This is what we print drone frames and load-bearing brackets in when the part really has to perform.

TPU Thermoplastic polyurethane FDM

Flexible, grippy, and made to bounce back.

A rubber-like filament that bends and rebounds without cracking. Excellent abrasion resistance and grip. We reach for it whenever a part needs to flex, seal, cushion or hold on.

Quick pick

If you only know one thing about your part…

It just needs to look right on a desk→PLA or Resin
It has to survive being used and handled→PETG
It lives outdoors or in the sun→ASA
It needs to be tough and take some heat→ABS
It must be light, stiff and load-bearing→PA-CF
It needs the finest possible detail→SLA resin
It has to bend, seal or cushion→TPU
You're honestly not sure→Ask us — that's the job

Finishing & post-processing

How the part leaves the studio.

01

As-printed

Cleaned and supports removed, layer lines visible. The honest, fastest, lowest-cost finish.

02

Sanded & smoothed

Layer lines knocked back by hand for a cleaner surface, ready for paint or a softer feel.

03

Primed & painted

Filled, primed and colour-matched — for presentation models and finished products.

04

Multi-colour

True colour printed into the part itself — logos, labels and gradients, no painting needed.

05

Inserts & hardware

Heat-set threaded inserts, magnets and brass fittings installed for parts that get assembled.

06

Engraving & lithophane

Engraved text and photo-relief lithophanes for awards, keepsakes and personalised gifting.
