Akara Labs is a prototyping and product-engineering studio in Chandigarh — a place where serious engineering meets an Indian workshop's care for form.

आकारā · kā · ra — form

The name

“Akara” is Sanskrit for form.

It means the act of giving shape to what does not yet exist — which is, exactly, what we do. Some of those forms are commercial. Some are personal. Some are someone's first prototype. Some will fly.

We treat every one — a drone frame for a defence startup or a custom award for a school — with the same care for how things are made.

The background

Built by an engineer, not a print shop.

Akara Labs is founded by an engineer with hands-on work across AI, IoT, robotics, drones, defence systems, industrial automation and smart devices — in CAD, 3D printing, electronics and embedded systems.

That depth is the point. When you send us a part, it's understood — not just dropped into a queue. We can talk through tolerances, materials, vibration, heat, and the trade-offs that decide whether a prototype works or wastes a week.

The studio is built to grow with the people it serves — from a founder's very first prototype to repeatable small-batch runs — without ever treating a job like just another line in a queue.

A studio that treats every prototype — whether it's a drone frame or a child's first award — with the same care for form and function.

How we work with you

A few commitments we don't bend on.

01

Your IP is yours

NDAs are welcome and signed gladly. Your files, designs and ideas are never reused, resold, or shown to anyone else.

02

Honest about what's possible

If printing is the wrong call for your part, we'll say so — and point you toward what's actually right.

03

A quote within 12 hours

With a clear breakdown of cost, material and lead time — and the trade-offs behind each, so you can decide fast.

04

Made in-house

Your part is designed and printed under one roof, on machines we calibrate ourselves — never silently outsourced.

Every part is printed on calibrated, well-maintained machines, and we grow our capacity deliberately alongside the work — so batch sizes scale up without ever losing the per-part care.
